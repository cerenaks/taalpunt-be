Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

TaalPunt is built to collect as little as possible. This page explains what we store and why.

What we collect

  • Email address and password, to create your account. Sign-in is handled by Amazon Cognito; we never see your password.
  • Your progress: XP, streak, completed lessons and stars, daily activity, and the questions and words you got wrong. This lets your progress follow you to a new phone.
  • Your local date, so streaks follow your calendar day.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no tracking across other apps or sites.
  • No location, contacts, photos or microphone data.
  • No usage analytics at this time. If we add privacy-friendly analytics later, this page will be updated first.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored on Amazon Web Services in Stockholm, Sweden (EU), encrypted at rest. Server logs do not contain your answers or email address. Pronunciation uses the built-in iPhone voice on your device.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. AWS processes it on our behalf to run the app.

Retention and deletion

Session history is removed automatically after a year. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Delete account); this permanently removes your account and all progress. Backups are kept for a short period and then expire.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask to access, correct or delete your data. Deleting your account in the app covers deletion; for anything else, contact us via the support page.

Children

TaalPunt is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
