Support

Questions, bugs or feedback? Email us at taalpunt.support@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

I did not get my confirmation code

Check your spam folder, then request a new code on the sign-in screen.

I forgot my password

Use “Forgot password” on the sign-in screen to reset it by email.

My progress is missing on a new phone

Sign in with the same email address. Progress is saved to your account and syncs after you sign in.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all progress.
